Privacy notice — Didattica Inclusiva AI

Version 2 · effective from 23 November 2026

Who is the controller

The controller of the data handled by the app's online services is Gianluca Demontis, Sardinia (Italy), email info@gianlucademontis.xyz.

The data a teacher enters on their own phone (profiles, stories, notes) remains under the control of the teacher or the school, which is its controller: the app does not send it to us.

What data stays on the phone

  • Student profiles (ID or pseudonym, class, difficulties, assistive tools, observation notes).
  • Social stories, app preferences.

They are saved in an encrypted store (SQLCipher, key protected by the Android Keystore), excluded from Android's automatic backup and deleted when the app is uninstalled. Photos taken for text recognition are processed on the phone and are neither saved nor sent. Pictures you add to story cards are copied, resized, into the app's private storage (not accessible to other apps), excluded from backup and deleted when the app is uninstalled; they are not encrypted like the store, so avoid photos in which the student can be recognised.

We recommend never entering first names, surnames or other data that identify the student: the app uses IDs or pseudonyms and warns you if the text seems to contain personal data (emails, tax codes, phone numbers, dates).

What data leaves the phone, and why

Service Data Purpose When
Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Functions, Firestore, App Check) — EU region anonymous ID generated by the app, generation counter, date of last use, subscription status run the built-in AI, apply usage limits, protect the service from abuse only if you use the built-in AI
Google Vertex AI (Gemini) — EU multi-region the prompt text shown in the preview (text to adapt and, from the profile, only the type of difficulty, assistive tools and presentation rules: never ID, class, notes or other data) generate the adapted material only when you press "Generate" and after accepting the notice
Google Play subscription purchase data (purchase token) verify and activate the Pro subscription, including after a reinstall, and prevent the same subscription from being used by several people only if you subscribe

Image prompts for stories: the app does not send them to anyone. You copy them, share them with an app you choose (for example Gemini, ChatGPT or Meta AI) or save them as a file (for example on Google Drive); from then on the terms of the chosen service apply. Before they leave, the app replaces names found in the story with "the main character" and shows you the text to check.

Sign language: the button opens the Spread the Sign dictionary (European Sign Language Centre) in your browser and sends it only the word you choose; from then on that site's privacy policy applies.

Prompts sent to the AI are not saved on our servers or recorded in logs and, under Google Cloud's terms, are not used to train the models. Google acts as data processor.

"Copy" and "Open in Gemini / ChatGPT" pass the text to another app of your choice: in that case, that app's privacy notice applies.

Legal basis

  • Performance of the requested service (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) for the built-in AI, quotas and subscription.
  • Legitimate interest in the security of the service (Art. 6(1)(f)) for App Check and usage limits.

Prompt texts may concern the educational needs of a minor: for this reason the app sends only anonymous educational descriptions and asks you not to enter identifying data. Schools using the app as part of their activities assess its use according to their own procedures (record of processing, DPIA where appropriate).

How long

  • Generation counters, date of last use and subscription status: as long as you use the app; you can delete them at any time.
  • For each subscription: a non-reversible fingerprint (hash) of the purchase token and the number of transfers between devices, no longer linked to you after deletion; they serve to prevent abuse and remain as long as the subscription exists.
  • Prompts: not kept (temporary processing).
  • Data on the phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability (Arts. 15-22 GDPR) by writing to info@gianlucademontis.xyz. In the app: Settings › Privacy › "Delete my data from the server" immediately deletes the anonymous ID, counters, date of last use and link to the subscription. You can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (www.garanteprivacy.it) or with the supervisory authority of your country.

Minors

The app is intended for teachers and is not aimed at minors. The "Show to the student" mode is for presenting stories in class under the teacher's supervision and does not collect data.

Changes

Any changes will be published on this page and notified in the app.
