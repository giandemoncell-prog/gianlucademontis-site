How it works

Production runs as an automated pipeline: a Raspberry Pi 5 running 24/7 picks the genre based on real listening data, generates lyrics and music, triggers on-demand video rendering on a GPU-equipped PC and publishes to YouTube, Facebook and Instagram. Editorial decisions stay with Gianluca Demontis.

Transparency about AI

Vocals, instruments and arrangements are generated with artificial intelligence models (Suno for audio). We don't hide it: it is stated on this site, on the YouTube channel and on the official MusicBrainz entry.

Frequently asked questions

Who is behind Bachata Vibes Music?

Bachata Vibes Music was founded and is run by Gianluca Demontis, an AI developer and trainer based in Sardinia, Italy. Production is automated; editorial direction is his.

Is the music generated with artificial intelligence?

Yes. Vocals, instruments and arrangements are generated with AI models (Suno for audio), and we say so openly. Cover art and videos are produced automatically and branded for each track.

Where can I listen to Bachata Vibes Music?

Individual tracks and Mega Mixes are on YouTube; the "Bachata Vibes Music Collection" albums are on Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer and other digital stores.

Can I get backing tracks or a jingle for my dance school or business?

Yes. Bachata Vibes Music produces on request step-counting backing tracks for dance schools and branded jingles for businesses. Contact Gianluca Demontis through the site's contact section.
