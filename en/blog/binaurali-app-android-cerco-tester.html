Binaurali, my binaural beats app: looking for 12 Android testers

Over the past months I've listened to a lot of binaural beats: in the evening to relax, during the day to focus while working. In the end I built myself an app to listen to them on my phone, simply. It's called Binaurali. Before releasing it to everyone on Google Play, I need your help.

What Binaurali is

  • 11 binaural beats tracks for relaxation, meditation, study and winding down before sleep.
  • Works without internet: all tracks are already inside the app.
  • No account, no ads, no data collected.
  • Playlist to play several tracks in a row and a sleep timer that stops playback on its own.
  • Keeps playing with the screen off, with controls in the notification.

You need headphones: binaural beats only work when each ear gets its own sound. Note: for now the app's interface is in Italian only, but it's very simple to use.

Why I need testers

Google requires a new app to be tested by at least 12 people for 14 days in a row before it can be published for everyone. I'm not asking you to use it for hours every day. All I need is that you:

  1. install it on an Android phone;
  2. keep it installed for 14 days;
  3. try it now and then and tell me if something doesn't work.

It's free, of course, and it will stay that way.

How to join (2 minutes)

  1. Join the testers group with your Google account (the one on your phone): groups.google.com/g/binaurali-tester
  2. Accept the test invitation on this page, with the same account: test page on Google Play
  3. Install Binaurali from the Play Store: after joining you'll find it by searching "Binaurali", or through the link on the page in step 2.

If the Play Store doesn't show it right away, wait a few minutes and try again: group membership can take a while to activate.

A word of caution

Binaurali is meant for relaxation and focus. It is not a medical device and does not replace a doctor for insomnia, anxiety or other conditions. If you have epilepsy or wear a device such as a pacemaker, ask your doctor first.

Feedback

Write to me at giandemoncell@gmail.com. I'm especially interested in:

  • whether the timer stops the music at the right time;
  • whether the music keeps playing with the screen off;
  • which phone you use, if something doesn't work.

Thanks to everyone who lends a hand. When the app goes public, testers will be the first to know.
