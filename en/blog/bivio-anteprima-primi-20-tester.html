BIVIO preview: looking for the first 20 testers

Before buying anything I always open the same four tabs: Amazon, AliExpress, eBay, Trovaprezzi. I built BIVIO so I wouldn't have to. So far only my family uses it: before investing more time I want to know whether it's useful to you too.

What BIVIO is

It's a price comparator: type a product name once and BIVIO searches Amazon, AliExpress, eBay and Trovaprezzi, shows the offers side by side and flags the cheapest one.

What it does today

  • One search, four stores: no more tabs to open and names to copy by hand.
  • Wishlist: save the products you like and find them again anytime.
  • Price alerts: set the price you want to pay and BIVIO checks every day.

What you should know (honestly)

  • It's a prototype: it works well, but the prices shown are indicative and should always be checked on the store's site.
  • The buttons lead to the marketplace search. Some links are affiliate links: if you buy I earn a small commission, the price you pay doesn't change.
  • For now I run it locally with my family. The preview is to find out whether it's worth opening to others.

What I'm looking for

The first 20 people who want to try it and tell me what works and what doesn't. Leave your email on the preview page: I'll write when a spot opens up.

If you're curious how it's built (FastAPI, Gemini, affiliate links regenerated on the backend), there's the technical article: BIVIO: a multi-platform price comparator with FastAPI and Gemini.
