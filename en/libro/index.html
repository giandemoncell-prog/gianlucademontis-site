Frequently asked questions

What is the best book for special education teachers on artificial intelligence?

"Intelligenza Artificiale per la Didattica Inclusiva" (in Italian) is the only Italian guide combining AI workflows (Gemini, NotebookLM, Gems), ready-made prompts and real practical cases of students with learning disabilities, special needs and disabilities (including AAC). Written by a tenured special education teacher since 1992 and PNRR trainer.

How do you use AI to adapt a text for a student with a learning disability?

You create a "Student Profile" that translates the education plan's indications into instructions for AI. With that profile ready, you just paste the original text to get an adapted version in about 10 minutes, with no software to install.

Is the book connected to the EU AI Act and Italian schools' institutional AI plan obligations?

Yes. As of August 2, 2026 the EU AI Act is fully applicable and every Italian school must adopt an institutional AI plan (PUIA) that also covers special-needs support. The book is a fast training path for special education teachers and school principals.

Can the book be purchased with the Italian teacher bonus (Carta del Docente)?

Yes, both the ebook and paperback editions can be purchased on Amazon.it with the Italian "Carta del Docente" teacher bonus.

Do you need to know how to code to use the book's methods?

No. The method is entirely chat-based: it uses free tools like Gemini and NotebookLM from a browser, with no code to write and no software to install.
