Frequently asked questions

How much does PNRR training cost for my school?

The cost depends on the number of modules and the format (in-person workshop or online course). After the free introductory call you receive a written proposal that can be reported against the Italian teacher training bonus (Carta del Docente) or PNRR funds.

What does the automation audit for SMEs and public administration include?

An analysis of your office's or company's repetitive processes to identify where automation (n8n workflows, generative AI) saves real time, followed by an implementation proposal with team training for independence after delivery.

How much does it cost to install a local AI on my computer?

The cost depends on the available hardware and the intended use (chat, writing, coding assistance). The initial setup is a one-time cost: no recurring monthly subscription after installation.

Do I need an internet connection to use a locally installed AI?

No. A locally installed AI model runs entirely on the device and also works offline — useful for professional practices without a reliable connection, or for anyone who can't send sensitive data to external servers.

Is the introductory call really free and with no obligation?

Yes. It's 20 minutes to understand together whether and how I can help with your specific situation — no cost, no obligation to continue.
