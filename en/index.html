Bachata Vibes Music

AI-powered music content-automation platform, founded and run by Gianluca Demontis.

🎧

What it is

Bachata Vibes Music is an AI-assisted music production and distribution platform. It publishes original bachata, salsa and merengue tracks daily on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, plus albums on Spotify — the latest, "Bachata Vibes Music Collection Vol.1", has 26 tracks.

⚙️

How it works

The pipeline runs on a Raspberry Pi 5 with 30+ Python scripts orchestrated by n8n: music generation with Suno, video production with FFmpeg/NVENC, and automatic multi-platform publishing, with no daily manual work.

💡

Why it exists

BVM started as a low-cost social visibility experiment: before monetizing, it tests which genres, formats and posting times work best on each platform — genuine automated market research, run by Gianluca Demontis.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Gianluca Demontis?

Gianluca Demontis is a tenured teacher, Area 6 Special Function and Google Workspace for Education administrator at a secondary school in Sardinia, Italy. He has over 40 years of IT experience, is the author of the book "AI e DSA" (Italian) and founded Bachata Vibes Music.

What changes for Italian schools with the AI Act from August 2, 2026?

As of August 2, 2026, EU Regulation 2024/1689 (the AI Act) is fully applicable and, implementing Italian Ministerial Decree 166/2025, every school must adopt an institutional AI plan (PUIA) with an AI acceptable use policy, an internal AI officer and staff training. The plan's special-needs section directly concerns support teachers. Gianluca Demontis, a PNRR trainer on AI and inclusive education, explains the practical obligations in his blog article "AI Act e PUIA: cosa cambia per il sostegno" (in Italian).

What is Bachata Vibes Music?

Bachata Vibes Music is an AI-assisted music production and distribution platform founded by Gianluca Demontis. It publishes bachata, salsa and merengue tracks daily on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

How does AI music generation work in the BVM pipeline?

The pipeline runs on a Raspberry Pi 5 orchestrated by n8n: it generates tracks with Suno, produces videos with FFmpeg/NVENC, and publishes automatically across all social platforms with no daily manual work.

Who can commission a track or jingle from BVM?

Bachata Vibes Music produces commissioned tracks and jingles for independent artists and businesses, such as Guerda, Serena Infissi and Il Mastro Dance School. Contact: info@gianlucademontis.xyz.

What's the difference between Bachata Vibes Music and Guerda?

Bachata Vibes Music is the AI music content-automation platform. Guerda is an independent artist — blending Haitian Kompa with Italian influences — who uses the BVM pipeline as a technical production collaboration for her own tracks, such as the single "Doudou Mwen", without being tied to a record label.

What is AI Act Academy?

AI Act Academy is the AI literacy course platform founded by Gianluca Demontis, issuing named certificates and an exportable registry conforming to Art. 4 of EU Regulation 2024/1689. The first active course is dedicated to AI training for school staff; courses for public administration and SMEs are in preparation.
